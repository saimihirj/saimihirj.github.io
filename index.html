About

I grew up in Bengaluru and did all of my schooling and college here, studying computer science with a focus on machine learning at PES University. While I was still a student I started a fitness company, and running it taught me more about customers, pricing and timing than any class did. I have been chasing that feeling of building something from nothing ever since.

My work has moved between three worlds that I have never really seen as separate. At ISB I spent close to two years on research into private equity deals and on how people and AI work together, and I taught MBA students along the way. At Check Point I built an AI agent that is now live across three teams, and at Cube I led go-to-market and India outreach, taking two products, Cite and StoreHQ, from zero to their first customers. Through The VC Fellowship and my scouting work, I have been learning to look at companies the way an investor does.

Friends call me a chameleon because I enjoy picking up a new tool, language or market and getting good at it in about a month. That habit of learning fast, and then building with what I learn, is the thread that runs through everything on this page.

arXiv 2025

AI-Human Collaboration as Multi-Agent Adaptation

With Prothit Sen. A revised version, When Should AI Follow? Task Structure and Joint Adaptation by Human and AI Agents, is under review at Management Science. Its central finding is that machine search adds the most value when it follows high-quality human judgment.
